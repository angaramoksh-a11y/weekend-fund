☀️ How we pick

Two lenses. No thesis-of-the-month.

We're truly generalist — probably more generalist than most investors who tell you they're generalist. Every investment just has to clear one of two bars:

01🌊

Consumer behavior shifts

Products that change how people behave in the world. Snapchat rewired how a generation communicates. Deel rewired how companies hire — from anywhere. We look for that same before-and-after.

02⚡

Technology shifts

New technology that lets founders build what was previously impossible — voice-first apps, AI-native products, and whatever ships next. When the tools change, the winners get rewritten.

So, what do you invest in?

Truly generalist — probably more generalist than most investors who tell you they're generalist. Consumer, B2B, fintech, health, AI, crypto, deep tech. If it could be someone's next favorite thing, we're curious.

Do you lead rounds?

Nope! We write $100K–$300K checks alongside great lead investors, at pre-seed and seed.

How picky are you?

We look at ~2,000 companies a year and invest in ~15. That's about 1% — but a warm, friendly 1%.

Are you hands-on?

We're not board-seat people, but we're not passive either. We build tools for founders, make a lot of introductions, and our 350+ operator LPs are an extension of our small team.
